Practice lap times come from OpenF1 and appear shortly after each session. Short run is each driver's best lap (or the sum of their best sectors, if lower) as a gap to the fastest; it feeds qualifying pace at 30%. Long run is the median of 5+ lap stints, adjusted for tyre compound, fuel and wear; it feeds race pace at 10%. These weights did best when tested on rounds 6–14. Change them with Practice weight on the Model tab.

Prices move after each race based on the average points per $1m over the last three races: rounded to 3 decimals, below 0.605 is a big drop, 0.605–0.9 a small drop, 0.9–1.195 a small rise, and 1.195 or more a big rise. Assets priced $18.5m and up move ±0.1/0.3m; cheaper ones move ±0.2/0.6m. The Needs columns show the points required this weekend to reach each band.

Each circuit's character feeds the simulation. Overtaking scales overtake points, Grid weight is how much the starting position decides the result, and Chaos scales retirements. These are starting estimates, so adjust them if you know better.

How it works

Each race weekend is simulated thousands of times. Qualifying order comes from each driver's recent qualifying positions plus random variation, with more spread in the midfield. The race blends the starting grid with the driver's recent race pace, weighted by the circuit's grid weight. It then adds retirements (the team's recent retirement rate), overtakes (each driver's 2026 overtake rate), the fastest lap, Driver of the Day and pit-stop points (each team's recent average).

Every simulated weekend is scored with the official 2026 rules. Those rules include qualifying 10–1, race 25–1, sprint 8–1, ±1 per place gained or lost (sprint losses capped at −10), −20 race and −10 sprint for not classifying, the constructor Q2/Q3 bonus and pit-stop bands. Form half-life sets how quickly older races stop counting. Form blend mixes in each asset's actual recent fantasy average, which picks up things the simulation can't see.

  • Transfers: 2 free per race, and 1 unused transfer carries over. Each extra transfer costs −10.
  • Chips: Wildcard and Limitless remove the transfer limit (Limitless also removes the budget cap for one race). x3 Boost triples your top driver and doubles your second. No Negative clips negative scoring categories to zero. Autopilot gives the Boost to whichever of your drivers scores the most.
  • Price rises are shown but don't affect the Transfers ranking, which is based on points.
