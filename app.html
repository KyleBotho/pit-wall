Practice pace

From OpenF1 lap times, shortly after each session. Short run = best lap or best-sector sum as a gap to the fastest; it feeds qualifying pace at 30%. Long run = median of 5+ lap stints adjusted for tyre, fuel and wear; it feeds race pace at 10%. Weights backtested on rounds 6–14; change them with Practice weight in Model.

How it works

Each weekend is simulated thousands of times. Qualifying order comes from recent qualifying positions (blended with practice pace when available) plus random variation. The race blends the grid with recent race pace by the circuit's grid weight, then adds retirements (team's recent rate), overtakes (2026 rate), fastest lap, Driver of the Day and pit-stop points.

Every weekend is scored with the official 2026 rules: qualifying 10–1, race 25–1, sprint 8–1, ±1 per place (sprint losses capped at −10), −20 race / −10 sprint for not classifying, the constructor Q2/Q3 bonus and pit-stop bands.

  • Transfers: 2 free per race; 1 unused carries over. Extras cost −10.
  • Chips: WC and LL remove the transfer limit (LL also the budget). X3 triples your top driver and doubles the second. NN clips negative categories. AP gives the Boost to your highest scorer.
